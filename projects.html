Flagship project

Chicago Crime & Safety Analysis

Business problem

Studying neighborhood-level crime patterns in Chicago and whether socioeconomic indicators help explain variation in crime rates.

Approach

Built a 4-stage pipeline on real data: ingestion from the Chicago Data Portal and U.S. Census (~1.48M records) → cleaning & transformation → SQL-based analysis → a Tableau dashboard for exploration.

Key finding

Socioeconomic indicators showed only a weak relationship with crime rate (R² ≈ 0.03) — reported honestly as a weak result rather than oversold as a strong correlation.

Why this one: I live in Chicago, so I wanted to actually dig into my own city's data instead of a generic dataset.
