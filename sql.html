Flagship project

Chicago Crime & Safety Analysis

Business problem

Studying neighborhood-level crime patterns in Chicago and whether socioeconomic indicators help explain variation in crime rates.

Approach

SQL-based analysis over ~1.48M records combining the Chicago Data Portal with U.S. Census data, feeding into a Tableau dashboard.

Key finding

Socioeconomic indicators showed only a weak relationship with crime rate (R² ≈ 0.03) — reported honestly rather than oversold.

Why this one: I live in Chicago, so I wanted to actually dig into my own city's data.

Olist E-Commerce Analytics Engineering (dbt)

Business problem

An online marketplace needs trustworthy order, seller and customer tables, and wants to know how much late deliveries cost in customer satisfaction.

Approach

Modeled 9 raw sources (99,441 real orders) with dbt and DuckDB through staging, intermediate and mart layers into a star schema: two fact tables at different grains and four dimensions. Covered by 81 data tests, 2 dbt unit tests and reconciliation checks between layers, with GitHub Actions CI.

Key findings

Late deliveries are 6.7% of orders but 32.5% of all 1–2 star reviews (2.27 vs 4.29 stars). The late rate tripled around Black Friday and in Feb–Mar 2018, and same-state shipments arrive in about half the time (7.9 vs 15.0 days).
