A little more about me

Hi, I'm Mallika — a data analytics & data engineering practitioner based in Chicago, currently completing my MS in Management Information Systems at the University of Illinois Chicago.

My background spans data analytics and data engineering: building dashboards people actually use, and pipelines that hold up outside of a notebook. I'm equally comfortable writing a complex SQL query, modeling data in Power BI or Tableau, or orchestrating a pipeline with Airflow and Spark on AWS.

Right now I'm sharpening core fundamentals — SQL, data structures & algorithms, and data engineering concepts from the ground up — while working toward the AWS Certified Data Engineer – Associate credential and applying to Data Analyst, BI Analyst, and Data Engineer roles.

I like projects that start with a real question rather than a dataset looking for a use — that's why you'll see me caveat a weak finding instead of overselling it. I'd rather be honest about what the data does and doesn't show.
