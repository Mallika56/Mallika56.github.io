Flagship project

Chicago Crime & Safety Analysis

Business problem

Studying neighborhood-level crime patterns in Chicago and whether socioeconomic indicators help explain variation in crime rates.

Approach

The final stage of a 4-stage pipeline: SQL-analyzed data (~1.48M records, Chicago Data Portal + Census) brought into an interactive Tableau dashboard for exploration by neighborhood and time period.

Key finding

Socioeconomic indicators showed only a weak relationship with crime rate (R² ≈ 0.03) — reported honestly rather than oversold.

Why this one: I live in Chicago, so I wanted a dashboard I could actually explore about my own city.
