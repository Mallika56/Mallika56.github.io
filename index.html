About Me

I've always been curious about how things work and why they work the way they do. I enjoy asking questions, figuring things out, and learning something new along the way. I think that's what eventually led me toward data.

I started with a background in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering, where I developed an interest in artificial intelligence and machine learning. That interest grew over time and brought me to Chicago, where I pursued my Master's in Management Information Systems at the University of Illinois Chicago.

Most of my time is spent working with data, writing code, building dashboards, or trying to figure out why something isn't working the way I expected. I enjoy working with Python, SQL, Tableau, Power BI, and AWS, but what I find most interesting is understanding what the data is actually telling us.

Through my work at UIC, I've had the opportunity to work with startup data, business reports, and marketing analytics. I've also explored natural language processing and machine learning through my research on emotional suppression in men's online conversations. It's a project that started with a simple question and turned into something I genuinely enjoyed working on.

Outside of academics and work, I enjoy exploring new things, learning at my own pace, and getting involved in different communities. I like meeting people, hearing their stories, and occasionally stepping away from my laptop to enjoy everything else life has to offer.

I'm still learning, experimenting, and figuring out where I want to take my interests next. This website is a small collection of what I've worked on so far and the things I've learned along the way.

Feel free to look around!
